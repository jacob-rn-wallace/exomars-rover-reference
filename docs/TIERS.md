# Status and tier definitions

**Status**
- *stated*: the number is printed in a source (a paper, a label on a drawing, an ESA data file).
- *derived*: our measurement, or arithmetic on stated numbers (for example a sum of two labelled dimensions, or a size measured on an image or reconstruction).
- *inferred*: our reading or assumption.
- *unknown*: nothing usable.

**Uncertainty** is our estimate of the likely error at 1:1, not a published tolerance.

**Tier**
- **A**: use as an anchor.
- **B**: fine for an interpretation or a scale model; check before relying on it.
- **C**: rough; treat as a guide.
- **D**: unknown; decide it yourself or find a source.

The column 'At 1:4' in `data/dimensions.md` is the 1:1 value divided by four; the uncertainty at 1:4 is a quarter of the 1:1 figure.
