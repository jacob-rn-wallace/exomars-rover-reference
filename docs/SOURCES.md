# Sources (public)

Cited for the facts they state. Nothing from them is reproduced here.

- ESA SPICE Service, *ExoMarsRSP Operational SPICE Kernel Dataset*, https://doi.org/10.5270/esa-uvyv4w5.
- Vago et al., Astrobiology 17(6-7):471-510 (2017), doi 10.1089/ast.2016.1533 (open access, CC BY 4.0): Fig. 4 with general dimensions in mm; wheel and mass figures in the text.
- Coates et al., 'The PanCam Instrument for the ExoMars Rover', Astrobiology 17(6-7):511-541 (2017), doi 10.1089/ast.2016.1548 (CC BY 4.0).
- Altieri et al., 'Investigating the Oxia Planum subsurface with the ExoMars rover and drill', Advances in Space Research 71:4895-4903 (2023), doi 10.1016/j.asr.2023.01.044: drill parts and tool diameter.
- Herve et al., 'The WISDOM radar on board the ExoMars 2022 Rover: Characterization and calibration of the flight model', Planetary and Space Science 189 (2020) 104939; Benedix et al., 'The ExoMars 2028 WISDOM antenna assembly: Description and characterization', Planetary and Space Science 253 (2024) 105995: WISDOM antenna size and height.
- Ciarletti et al., 'WISDOM GPR Designed for Shallow and High-Resolution Sounding of the Martian Subsurface', Proceedings of the IEEE 99(5):824-836 (2011): antenna tilt in the 2011 design (the ESA kernels give 8 degrees).
- Ferrando et al. (Leonardo), 'Photovoltaic Assemblies for the Power Generation of the Exomars Missions', E3S Web of Conferences 16, 04002 (2017), doi 10.1051/e3sconf/20171604002: solar array layout and cell counts.
- Whetsel and Salvo, NASA Mars 2018 mission formulation slide 'ESA ExoMars Rover' (2011): stowed envelope.
- AZUR SPACE, datasheet '3G30C - Advanced, 30% Triple Junction GaAs Solar Cell' (2016): cell outline (probably the rover's cell; not confirmed).
- Patel, Slade and Clemmet, 'The ExoMars rover locomotion subsystem', Journal of Terramechanics 47(4):227-242 (2010), doi 10.1016/j.jterra.2010.02.004; Michaud et al., 'Development of the ExoMars Chassis and Locomotion Subsystem', i-SAIRAS 2008 (earlier designs, context only).
- ESA ExoMars factsheet and ESA rover pages and image captions (https://www.esa.int): overall size, mass, the two UHF antennas.
- Josset et al., 'The Close-Up Imager Onboard the ESA ExoMars Rover: Objectives, Description, Operations, and Science Validation Activities', Astrobiology 17(6-7):595-611 (2017), doi 10.1089/ast.2016.1546: CLUPI size and its calibration target (nine 6.5 mm patches on a 25 x 25 mm surface).
- Laneve et al., 'ExoMars Rover Module: Verification of the Loop Heat Pipes thermal performance in system level testing', 50th International Conference on Environmental Systems (2021): names of the rover's external parts (ALD radiators, UHF antennas) and the stated radiator plate areas.
- Korablev et al., 'Infrared Spectrometer for ExoMars: A Mast-Mounted Instrument for the Rover', Astrobiology 17(6-7):542-564 (2017), doi 10.1089/ast.2016.1543: ISEM optical box size.
- Riva et al., 'The ExoMars Rover Solar Array Assembly', 2019 European Space Power Conference, doi 10.1109/ESPC.2019.8932077: solar array cell layout.
- Azkarate et al., 'Concept, Development and Testing of Mars Rover Prototypes for ESA Planetary Exploration', IEEE Robotics and Automation Magazine (2021): the half-scale ESA lab rovers ExoTeR and MaRTA (context only).
